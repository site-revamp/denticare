Helpful before you visit

A little clarity
goes a long way.

For advice about your own symptoms, treatment suitability or appointment needs, contact the clinic.

Is my appointment enquiry confirmed immediately?
No. The general enquiry form states that your preferred slot is subject to availability and staff will contact you within five working days. Call the concierge for an urgent appointment request.
Why is my former Denticare clinic now called Royce?
Some former Denticare branches have been rebranded to Royce Dental. Use the current branch name and contact details when arranging your visit. The official Royce website provides its current clinic listings.
Can I use subsidies or MediSave?
Eligibility depends on the scheme, treatment and applicable conditions. Review Denticare’s fee information and ask the clinic to confirm what applies to your circumstances.
Are concierge hours the same as clinic hours?
No. The hours below are for Denticare Concierge. Confirm opening times directly with your preferred clinic.
