# Denticare — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Denticare is a Singapore dental-care brand serving individuals and families since 1971. Its network offers general dentistry, restorative treatment, orthodontics and oral surgery, alongside corporate dental programmes.

The redesigned homepage supports families, patients exploring treatment and corporate clients through clearer clinic information, distinct appointment channels and an organised content hierarchy.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Consistent Clinic Network Information

Verified branch names and clear Royce labels replace inconsistent network information, without using an unconfirmed headline clinic count. This addresses the conflicting totals of 14 clinics on the previous homepage and 17 on the corporate page, while accounting for rebranded branches.

### 2. Clearer Appointment Channels

“Choose a clinic and book” is separated from appointment enquiries, with a visible telephone route for urgent appointment requests. Previously, booking links appeared alongside a general request form stating a response within five working days. The revised presentation clarifies the difference between booking and submitting an enquiry.

### 3. A Logical Heading Structure

One descriptive H1 introduces the homepage, followed by logical section headings and statistics presented as supporting text. This replaces eight H1 headings that included numerical statistics and section titles, establishing a clearer content hierarchy.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual hierarchy:** The main heading, section titles and supporting statistics have distinct roles.
- **Consistency:** Clinic names and brand labels distinguish Denticare branches from rebranded Royce locations.
- **User experience:** Separate booking and enquiry routes make appointment actions easier to understand.
- **Layout:** Structured clinic and contact information creates a clearer progression towards choosing and contacting a clinic.
